# Demo, 10 December 2026 — pyArchInit ↔ a StratiGraph room

**Goal:** show, from QGIS, a site going into a room on a StratiGraph node and
coming back — whole records, photos, drawings and excavation plans — and two
people working on the same room.

Slides (English, with screenshots of the rehearsal): the «pyArchInit ↔
StratiGraph — Demo 10 Dec 2026» deck.

**Before the demo**
- pyArchInit **5.19.x** (s3dgraphy 1.6.0.dev144), EMStudio **1.6.0-dev.155** on the `Stratigraph_00001` branch; QGIS open on a
  database with the sample site («Scavo archeologico», 50 SU, 153 photos,
  57 excavation plans in `pyunitastratigrafiche` / `_usm`).
- A second, **empty** pyArchInit database (SQLite or PostgreSQL) created from
  the configuration window — the "other computer". Its GIS layers are empty
  too.
- The node address and an account on it (CNR test node; the dev-stack
  `https://em.localhost:8443/em` as fallback, started with
  `./fcn-up.sh --source --no-studio` so that its web pages answer). If WP4 has
  imported the `pyarchinit` client, set
  `pyArchInit/stratigraph/client_id = pyarchinit` (see
  `keycloak-client-pyarchinit.md`); otherwise the node's own native client is
  used.
- Pick a new room id (e.g. `demo-10-dicembre`): a room already used carries
  the previous run.
- Optional: EMStudio installed and opened once, to open the room from its
  link (the graph shows; photos and plans in the room do not yet — see below).

**The script** (StratiGraph Sync panel → *Send site to a room…*)

| # | Action | What the audience sees | Rehearsal (dev-stack, 27 Nov) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Sign in…* | the node's own login page in the browser; back in QGIS the window shows the user and «may create rooms» | — |
| 2 | *Plan only*, then *Send site* | the derived fields recomputed, the plan (files to upload, the database kept as reference), then 1,332 operations applied, 51 whole records, 153 photos and 56 excavation plans; the room link | 1.8 s + 4.7 s |
| 3 | Switch to the empty database, *Compare with room* | «no records here yet: nothing to send, the pull will bring the room» and what it will bring | 1.7 s |
| 4 | *Pull from room* | 50 SU with their whole record, relations and periodisation; 150 photos with thumbnails; 1 site geometry and 56 plans (500 polygons) written into the GIS layers; derived fields recomputed | 8.7 s |
| 5 | Show the SU layer in QGIS | the excavation plans drawn again, in the layer's CRS | — |
| 6 | Edit a field in the second database, *Compare with room*, *Send changes* | «1 field changed here, to send», then «1 applied, 1 field» | 2.2 s + 0.9 s |
| 7 | Back to the first database, *Compare with room*, *Pull from room* | «1 field changed in the room, the pull brings it»; photos, geometries and plans «already the same» | 2.7 s + 4.0 s |
| 8 | *Compare with room* again | 0 real differences, nothing to send | 2.2 s |

**Optional, if there is time:** edit the same field on both sides → the
compare shows a **conflict** with both values; *Pull from room* asks before
overwriting, and *Restore replaced values* puts the local one back.

**What to say about what is not there yet**
- Media of finds / pottery / tombs / structures stay local (outside
  StratiGraph's scope for now).
- 3 photos stay in the room: they belong to CON 500, a continuity node the
  export generates and the pull does not recreate.
- «To update» in a compare counts rows the import would rewrite with the same
  values; what matters is «0 real difference(s)».
- EMStudio opens the room and shows its graph, but not yet the photos and the
  GeoJSON plans: their address in the room's store has no file extension, and
  EMStudio recognises a picture by the extension (to report to its authors).
- Field↔cloud sync between nodes depends on D1–D5 with PSNC.
- Excavation plans follow E.D.'s shape (s3Dgraphy#44); the unit ↔ plan link
  (through the shared GeoJSON resource) waits for his confirmation.
